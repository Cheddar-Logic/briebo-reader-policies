# Briebo Reader — App Policies

Public policy documents for **briebo-reader**, an independent Reddit Devvit discussion discovery app.

- [Terms and Conditions](BRIEBO-READER-TERMS.md)
- [Privacy Policy](BRIEBO-READER-PRIVACY.md)

The reader's possible Reddit-to-Discord handoff, retention of Reddit titles, and AI-assisted planning have separate, default-disabled activation controls. Publishing these policy documents does **not** mean those features or external transfer have been enabled, nor that Reddit has approved a particular data-use purpose.

For privacy questions or concerns, [message the app operator on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose/?to=ajcolub).

These pages relate to Briebo Reader, not GitHub's own services or Reddit's official policies.
