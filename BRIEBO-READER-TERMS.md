## 1. What Briebo Reader does

Briebo Reader is a read-only discussion discovery application. Its initial scope is public posts in `r/devvit`; it can display a small selection of post titles and links inside Reddit for review. It is intended to help identify discussion topics, not certify that a post or statement is accurate.

The project is experimental. Features described as planned or optional in these terms are **not necessarily enabled**. In particular, automatic transfer to a private Discord channel, retaining Reddit post titles outside Reddit, and providing those titles to Briebo's autonomous AI planner are separately gated, pending review and approval.

Briebo Reader does not ask you to place wagers, does not provide verified research, and does not post, vote, comment or send private messages on behalf of Reddit users.
